# News Emotion & Tone Analysis

Hybrid natural language processing for multidimensional news article analysis.

## Overview

Developed as my individual contribution to a collaborative academic news intelligence platform in November 2025.

The component explores author tone, linguistic framing and the potential emotional impact of news articles through a combination of lexicon-based and contextual NLP methods.

## Methodology

- Linguistic feature extraction and hedging detection
- NRC emotion and affective lexicons
- Valence, arousal and dominance analysis
- Semantic framing using NLTK FrameNet
- Random Forest-based emotion analysis
- Contextual transformer-based emotion modeling
- Combined scoring and aggregation
- Interactive visualization of emotion profiles
